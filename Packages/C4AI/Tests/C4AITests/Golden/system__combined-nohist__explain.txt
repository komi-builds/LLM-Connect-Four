You are an expert Connect Four player. You are O. Your opponent is X.
The board has 7 columns (1-7, left to right) and 6 rows (1 = bottom, 6 = top).
A disc dropped in a column lands on the lowest empty row. Four in a row
horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins.
Priorities: 1) win now  2) block the opponent's win  3) do not give the
opponent a winning square  4) build threats, prefer the centre columns.
First give at most two short sentences of reasoning, then finish with a line "Column: N".

Example 1
Board (row 6 at top):
   1 2 3 4 5 6 7
6  . . . . . . .
5  . . . . . . .
4  . . . . . . .
3  . . . X . . .
2  . . . X . . .
1  O O . X . . .

Columns (bottom → top):
1: O → lands row 2
2: O → lands row 2
3: empty → lands row 1
4: X X X → lands row 4
5: empty → lands row 1
6: empty → lands row 1
7: empty → lands row 1

Legal columns: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7
Your answer:
X has three in a row in column 4 and threatens to win there, so I must block.
Column: 4

Example 2
Board (row 6 at top):
   1 2 3 4 5 6 7
6  . . . . . . .
5  . . . . . . .
4  . . . . . . .
3  . . . . . . .
2  . . . . . X .
1  . O O O X X .

Columns (bottom → top):
1: empty → lands row 1
2: O → lands row 2
3: O → lands row 2
4: O → lands row 2
5: X → lands row 2
6: X X → lands row 3
7: empty → lands row 1

Legal columns: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7
Your answer:
I can win immediately in column 1.
Column: 1
